# Benchmark methodology

Release evaluation must use independently reviewed, license-safe English and Simplified Chinese datasets with human and AI provenance, stable dataset hashes, and documented domain composition. Split evaluation by language, source domain, length band, native/non-native writing where lawful and ethical, model family, editing/paraphrase level, and mixed authorship.

## Evidence-informed reliability requirements

Market comparisons are useful only as research signals. A 2026 peer-reviewed study found Pangram strongest among Pangram, GPTZero, Copyleaks, and Turnitin on its 160-document academic experiment, but the authors still warned that detectors must not be sole evidence in high-stakes decisions. A University of Chicago working paper likewise found Pangram was the only tested detector to meet its stringent false-positive policy cap without losing detection performance. These results do not transfer automatically to DJL because Pangram is proprietary and the studies cover particular datasets.

The techniques worth reproducing are broader than any brand:

- train a supervised transformer on topic-, style-, and length-matched human/AI mirror pairs rather than relying only on perplexity;
- mine human false positives and their synthetic mirrors as progressively harder training examples;
- hold out domains, authors, prompt families, generator families, and future time periods;
- include humanization, paraphrase, translation, character perturbation, light AI polishing, and human/AI mixing;
- treat human, generated, AI-refined or mixed, and insufficient evidence as different outcomes;
- calibrate the deployed quantized artifact and optimize at a predeclared low false-positive operating point;
- report AUROC and average precision together with coverage, abstention, and confidence intervals; report Brier score and calibration error only for a score with validated probability semantics;
- continuously monitor newer generators and never use a detector result as proof of authorship.

Primary research:

- [University of Chicago false-positive policy-cap study](https://bfi.uchicago.edu/working-papers/artificial-writing-and-automated-detection/)
- [Pangram hard-negative mining with synthetic mirrors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14873)

Record model/tokenizer hashes, runtime version, preprocessing/calibration/segmentation versions, OS/architecture/CPU/memory, dataset hashes/counts/licenses, confusion matrices, per-class precision/recall/F1, uncertainty rate, eligible coverage, failures, latency percentiles, throughput, and peak RSS. Accuracy and performance runs must bypass persistent result-cache reads and writes, fail on any cache hit, and embed the exact model files, artifact fingerprint, output contract, and calibration bands in the report. Uncertain is an abstention, not silently a wrong or correct binary result. Publish both selective accuracy and coverage.

The HC3 evaluation source streams immutable-commit JSONL records into the harness without persisting source text. It uses paired human and ChatGPT answers, balances labels and languages, round-robins the two Chinese configurations, and records every source configuration and license. Calibration must use a disjoint sample from final release evaluation. The calibration partition and locked partition must have different row ranges and dataset digests; after thresholds are fixed, the locked partition must not be used for further tuning. A dataset consulted to accept or reject a candidate is validation evidence, not an untouched final holdout.

## TXD-22 V1 handling and limitations

[TXD-22 V1](https://doi.org/10.17632/prcjcggtjf.1) contains 66,000 rows across 22 source labels: one human source, 17 pure-AI sources, and four mixed or refined sources. The audited CSV has SHA-256 `232c260f8caf7ea61a331f39fa730956862afbe0c5fc554720daa07d215a2f7d`. Its columns are only `Question`, `Answer`, and `Source`; it has no human-author identifiers, exact generator model versions, or component-level provenance for mixed/refined answers. The audit also found 3,099 exact question strings and 81 duplicate `(Question, Source)` pairs. Therefore rows must be deduplicated and grouped by normalized-question hash before splitting; random row-level splitting is prohibited.

Grouping v2 applies Unicode NFKC, collapses every whitespace run to one space, trims, and case-folds before hashing. The sampler records both `grouping=txd22-question-nfkc-casefold-ws-v2` and a SHA-256 of the raw question in provenance, without emitting the prompt text, so audits can distinguish original string variants from their canonical prompt cluster. Buckets 0–5 are development and 6–9 are validation. The sampler intentionally exposes no TXD-22 locked role because the corpus has already been observed.

The 2026-07-25 fixtures used the earlier v1 trimmed-question hash. A retrospective v2 audit found three canonical prompt clusters crossing their declared roles. Case-folding also changes bucket assignment: among the 550 groups that would have occupied v2 buckets 8–9, 277 (`50.4%`) had already appeared in the old runs—105 in old development, 71 in old validation, and 101 in the old partition labeled locked. Consequently every TXD-22 partition emitted under v1 is observed validation evidence only, including the later scratch-hashing experiment inputs. Favorable v1 numbers are not clean split-generalization evidence; candidate-rejection conclusions remain valid safety evidence because neither leakage nor relabeling can turn a failed gate into a pass.

The 1,200-record TXD-22 partition originally labeled locked, with fixture SHA-256 `11be75ab67e33025c9f6ed1e8404adba688887880d0dacc5e6a99eb14031368a`, was opened on 2026-07-25 to accept or reject a frozen challenger policy. It and all other TXD-22 data must never again be described as untouched or reused as a final holdout. Grouping v2 prevents one canonical prompt from crossing its current development and validation roles, but it cannot prove author-disjoint evaluation because author identifiers are absent. The version record applies CC BY 4.0 to V1, while its public metadata alone does not establish the complete contributor-consent and provider-output rights chain. TXD-22 may support conditional internal calibration and observed challenger evaluation, but it is not sufficient evidence for a commercial accuracy claim.

Sequential validation must be disclosed in chronological order. Every partition inspected while choosing or rejecting a threshold becomes validation data, even if it was originally intended as a holdout. For the 2026-07-25 production-policy evaluation, HC3 offsets 0, 100, 200, and 300 were calibration or observed validation data; offset 400 remained untouched only until the frozen-policy run opened it. Subsequent challenger checks opened offsets 500 and 600. All of those windows are now observed; offset 600 in particular must not be called untouched or reused as a locked holdout.

Before/after comparisons must run the candidate and reconstructed legacy policy over the same extracted text and raw region scores. A frozen policy must classify the raw regions and reuse production's unique-character aggregation, largest-remainder percentage rounding, and 65% decision boundary; a percentile approximation is not sufficient at rounding edges. The benchmark must report the three-outcome confusion counts (AI, human, and abstention), false-positive and true-positive rates over all ground-truth examples, coverage, selective accuracy, and F1. Production harness schema 8 exposes the length-weighted 35th-percentile document score as a ranking statistic: AUROC, average precision, and fixed-threshold operating points are meaningful, but Brier score and expected calibration error are disabled because the statistic is not a calibrated probability. A separate evaluator may report Brier and calibration error for a directly normalized classifier probability with a reviewed label mapping; that interpretation must not be transferred to the production document statistic. The Hugging Face challenger evaluator is only a PyTorch research-ranking screen over raw text with one tokenizer truncation. It does not evaluate an exported ONNX/q8 artifact or the production extraction, routing, segmentation, aggregation, and cache path, so it cannot itself pass a release gate. Publish language, eligible-length, domain, generator, and attack/editing slices without silently dropping abstentions. Row-level Wilson intervals are descriptive unless the report establishes independent human authors, one evaluated human row per source group, and one evaluated AI row per prompt/source group; otherwise use author/prompt-disjoint sampling or cluster-robust inference. Zero observed false positives must always include the number of human samples and must not be described as zero population risk.

Human-LLM collaborative or AI-refined text is not a binary authorship label. Evaluate it as a separate class and report the full outcome distribution (likely AI, likely human, and inconclusive). Do not force these records into AI/human confusion counts or report binary accuracy, false-positive rate, recall, or F1 for them. Any claim about detecting AI refinement requires a representative, independently reviewed benchmark designed for that claim.

External datasets must record the immutable source revision, streamed-input digest, sample-selection rule, domain, generator, attack scenarios, and usage terms. Research-use data with no declared SPDX license may support internal evaluation but is not license evidence for redistribution or a general commercial accuracy claim.

Model selection has a separate provenance gate in [`model-license-audit.md`](./model-license-audit.md). A permissive license label on a model repository does not override noncommercial, educational-only, no-license, scraped, or proprietary sources in its training data. A high-scoring candidate with an unresolved chain remains research-only and cannot enter the production manifest.

Run the checked-in harness twice and require identical predictions/percentages. The synthetic smoke fixture validates plumbing only. A production accuracy claim requires larger representative external English and Chinese corpora, documented thresholds, confidence intervals, domain/length/native-language and editing slices, false-positive review (especially student and non-native writing), and independent product/legal review.
