# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who are not developers and want to talk to AI agents from their phone. They are away from
their desk, often one-handed, checking in on work that runs on their Mac or in DJL Cloud. They do
not want to read commands, logs or reasoning. They want to ask, see who is answering, and read the
answer.

## Product Purpose

DJL on the phone is a companion to the DJL desktop app and DJL Cloud. It lets someone chat with one
agent or a group of agents (Claude, Codex, Gemini, Grok and others) that run on their paired Mac,
with replies arriving as they are written. Success is a person opening the app, sending a message
to a group, and understanding who replied without learning anything about how agents work.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exact, premium. The app should feel like a well-made native iOS 26 app: confident enough to
use almost no colour, and calm enough that the conversation is the only thing asking for attention.

## Anti-references

- Rainbow accent colours, coloured bubble pickers, purple or blue "AI" glows and gradients.
- Developer consoles on a phone: inline command output, reasoning dumps, tool-call accordions.
- Glass used as decoration on content. Glass is for controls that float over content.
- Mixed icon families and one-off corner radii that make screens look assembled from parts.

## Design Principles

1. The conversation is the interface. Chrome floats, recedes, and never competes with a reply.
2. Black, white, and the agents. The only colour on screen is the identity of who is speaking.
3. Show the answer, not the work. Progress is one calm status line; details stay on the desktop.
4. One obvious action per screen. Anything technical lives one step away, never in the main path.
5. Fast is a feature. Text appears as it is written and nothing else on the screen moves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for all text and controls in light and dark. Supports Dynamic Type and large text
without clipping, 44 pt minimum touch targets, VoiceOver and TalkBack labels on every control,
Reduce Motion (no decorative movement) and Reduce Transparency (glass becomes a solid surface).
State is never carried by colour alone.
