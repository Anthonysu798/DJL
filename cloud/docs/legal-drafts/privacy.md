# DJL Cloud Privacy Policy — draft for review

**Not approved or published.** Intended URL: https://slcor.com/privacy. Effective date will be set when approved. The operator's legal name, address, privacy contact, processing locations, and retention schedules must be confirmed before publication.

## Information DJL processes

- Account information, such as your name, email address, account identifiers, verification status, and sign-in method. Password authentication uses a password hash rather than a readable password.
- Session and device information, including device or browser identifiers, user-agent information, sign-in IP address, and session timestamps. Settings → Devices shows active sessions and linked devices where available.
- Content you send to hosted features, including prompts, uploaded files, AI responses, and saved conversations. Optional synchronized content is processed when you enable or use synchronization.
- Usage and billing information, such as credit usage, subscription status, transaction references, and invoices. Stripe processes checkout and payment information under its own applicable terms and privacy notice.
- Support communications and operational records needed to diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and protect the service.

This policy covers DJL Cloud. Local-only desktop activity and content remain a separate context: describe the actual destination whenever a feature sends local content to a hosted service.

## Why information is used

DJL uses information to authenticate accounts, provide requested AI features, maintain saved conversations and enabled synchronization, calculate usage and credits, process billing, respond to support requests, detect fraud or abuse, and maintain service security and reliability.

Prompts and relevant files are sent to the model provider needed to answer the request. That provider's processing and retention depend on the selected model and DJL's applicable provider agreement. A complete provider list and contract-specific retention details must be confirmed before this policy is published.

## Human review and model improvement — confirmation required

The requested interface notice says: “Chats may be reviewed and used to improve our AI models.” This draft does **not** treat that sentence as evidence that DJL trains models on customer conversations or that every model provider has the same data policy.

Before publication, the owner must confirm whether conversation review is performed, who can access content, its purposes, the retention period, whether DJL or providers use it for training, and any consent or opt-out controls. Do not publish an unrestricted training permission or a no-training promise without matching operational practices and provider contracts. Use of existing content for a new purpose requires appropriate notice and legal authority, including consent where applicable.

## Sharing and processing locations

Information is disclosed to the service providers needed for the features you use, which may include model, hosting, storage, email, and payment providers. Social sign-in exchanges the information necessary to authenticate you with the chosen identity provider. Content deliberately shared by link can be viewed by anyone with access to that link.

DJL may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and service security. Confirm recipients, countries, international-transfer safeguards, and any business-transfer clause before publication. This draft makes no unverified promise about data residency, selling data, or advertising use.

## Retention and deletion

Account deletion disables the account and signs out its sessions immediately, with permanent deletion scheduled after 30 days. Accounting records may need to be retained and anonymized instead of erased. Confirm backup retention, uploaded-file deletion, security-log retention, and legal retention requirements before publishing specific deadlines.

## Your choices and requests

You can review active devices, sign out, manage billing, and request account deletion in DJL Cloud. You can choose whether to use cloud features and avoid submitting sensitive content. Contact support@slcor.com for access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests. Rights, available exceptions, and response deadlines depend on applicable law.

DJL Cloud is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Contact support if you believe a child's information was submitted.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be communicated where required. Contact support@slcor.com with privacy questions. Confirm the responsible legal entity and dedicated privacy contact before publication.
