# Policy publication review

These are proposed texts for owner and legal review, not active policies. Both intended public URLs returned HTTP 404 on 2026-10-13. The auth interface uses the requested slcor.com links; publishing the policies remains a separate step after approval.

Before publication, confirm:

1. Operator legal name, address, jurisdiction, and effective date.
2. Actual human-review and training practices, consent controls, and model-provider contracts. The requested AI notice is preserved in the UI; its data-use wording still needs confirmation.
3. Hosting/processing countries, subprocessors, backup and security-log retention, and deletion of uploads.
4. Stripe renewal/cancellation settings, plan-credit expiration, top-up refund terms, taxes, and billing contact.
5. Privacy-request process and relevant consumer/privacy jurisdictions.

Sources informing the review flags:

- [OPC: AI, privacy, and your business](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/technology/artificial-intelligence/ai_business/) — processing needs legal authority and meaningful consent where consent is relied on.
- [FTC: Uphold privacy and confidentiality commitments](https://www.ftc.gov/policy/advocacy-research/tech-at-ftc/2024/01/ai-companies-uphold-your-privacy-confidentiality-commitments) — AI data-use claims must match actual practices.

Technical scope checked: cloud auth configuration, device/session registration, account deletion route, billing interface, and Phase 0 product spec. This review does not establish compliance in a particular jurisdiction.
